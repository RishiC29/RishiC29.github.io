About

I started building things through FIRST Robotics, designing and testing the shooter mechanism for Team 1325 through three competition seasons. That hands-on start pulled me toward Computer Engineering at McMaster, where I now split my time between research and racing — tracing requirements through GPU libraries at McMaster's Centre for Software Certification, and building the sensor and data logging systems for McMaster Baja Racing. Across all of it, I'm drawn to the point where hardware, firmware, and real-world testing meet. The full rundown of roles and coursework lives on my resume — this site is mostly a place to show what I've built.

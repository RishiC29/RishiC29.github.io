Ditto

CXC 2026 — AI Hackathon
Next.js TypeScript Three.js ElevenLabs Solana

Ditto is a digital twin that's meant to think, remember, and speak like its user rather than like a generic chatbot. People build their twin through a personality quiz, ongoing diary entries that become long-term memory, and a short voice recording used to clone their voice — then can hold conversations with it the way they would with themselves.

Our team of four built it with Next.js and TypeScript, with a Three.js avatar that lip-syncs to ElevenLabs-generated speech. Diary entries are embedded into a vector database for retrieval-augmented responses, and a hash-only verification layer on Solana confirms each twin's identity without storing personal data on-chain.

The project won Best Use of ElevenLabs at CXC 2026.

FRC Shooter — Team 1325

2022 – 2024
SOLIDWORKS Mechanical design FRC regulations

For the 2024 FIRST Robotics Competition, I designed and built the shooter for our robot — the component responsible for our performance at the World Championship, where we ranked 5th in our division. The design requirements were accuracy, consistency, speed, and reliability, with a mechanism that could adjust to different ranges and angles.

I sketched and modeled the shooter in SOLIDWORKS, then selected lightweight, durable materials to keep it effective while staying within the competition's weight limits. Each prototype was tested against shooting distance, accuracy, and reload time, with that feedback feeding directly back into the next revision.

Shooter CAD detail, and Team 1325 after winning the FIRST Ontario District Centennial College Event, 2024.

EarlyGoose

Hack the North 2023
React Tailwind CSS PoseNet

EarlyGoose is a habit-building website built around an animated goose avatar that reacts to whether the user is actually keeping up with their morning routine. The idea grew out of wanting an alarm clock that gets people out of bed, and turned into a lighthearted, gamified take on habit tracking rather than a typical productivity app.

Our team of four built it in React and Tailwind CSS over 36 hours, using PoseNet and TensorFlow to detect whether the user was still lying in bed. Most of the stack was new to us going in, learned on the fly over the course of the hackathon.

UTRA Hacks Robot

UTRA Hacks 2025
Arduino C++ Embedded firmware

Built for UTRA Hacks 2025, this robot tackled three autonomous challenges: color detection and flag placement, maze navigation, and color pattern recognition. It ran on an Arduino Uno R4 with JGA25-370 gearmotors, an L298 motor driver, and a TCS34725 RGB color sensor, and completed all three challenge courses.

I designed and developed the firmware control logic for the robot under strict real-time constraints, implementing embedded C++ drivers to interface the sensors, actuators, and microcontroller at the hardware level. Most of the debugging time went into timing issues, sensor noise, and non-deterministic behavior through iterative testing.

Racket Expression Evaluator

Grade 12 → First year
Python Parsing Algorithms

A Python interpreter that reads and evaluates Racket-style expressions written in prefix notation. It started as my Grade 12 Computer Science project: a solver that applied BEDMAS order of operations to prefix expressions, with error-checking to catch invalid input and keep computations accurate. I later revisited it to implement the order of operations through recursive parsing and structured expression evaluation, and added more robust exception handling for malformed input and edge cases.

Thumb Piano

Onshape Woodworking CAD

A kalimba (thumb piano) modeled in Onshape and built by hand. The plywood body has a dark wood inlay running down the front, with eight tuned steel tines held in place by a bridge and pressure bar over a support rod — the mechanism that lets each tine be plucked and re-tuned by adjusting how far it extends.
